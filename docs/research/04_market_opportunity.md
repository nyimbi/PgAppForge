### Africa ERP market (critical segment)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| MEA ERP market (2024) | $5.38B |
| MEA ERP market (2032) | $10.20B |
| CAGR | 8.7% |
| Africa share of global ERP | 2.7% |
| Africa share of global population | 18% |
| Penetration gap | 7× underserved |

**Why Africa ERP is underserved:**
1. Foreign ERP platforms assume Western banking (SWIFT, SEPA) — not mobile money
2. Foreign payroll assumes fixed salary + statutory deductions — not Kenya NSSF/NHIF, Uganda NSSF/PAYE
3. Foreign systems assume high-bandwidth connectivity — not 2G/3G edge environments
4. License costs in USD are prohibitive for local SMEs (Odoo Enterprise at $14.9K/year = 4× Kenya median annual business revenue)
5. No USSD support for feature phone users (still 60%+ of transactions in rural Africa)

### Key Africa ERP markets

**Kenya**
- GDP: $118B (2024)
- Formal businesses: 480,000+ registered
- ERP adoption: <15% (estimated)
- Key mandates: KRA eTIMS (electronic tax invoicing), KRA iTax integration, NSSF/NHIF statutory
- Mobile money: Safaricom M-Pesa processes KES 32T/year (~$250B)

**Nigeria**
- GDP: $477B (2024, largest Africa economy)
- Formal businesses: 1.3M+ registered
- ERP adoption: <10%
- Key mandates: FIRS e-invoicing, PENCOM pension, NSITF, NHF
- Fintechs: 200+ licensed (Central Bank of Nigeria)

**Uganda**
- GDP: $52B (2024)
- Key mandates: URA EFRIS (e-fiscal receipts), NSSF statutory
- Mobile money: MTN MoMo + Airtel dominant

**Ghana**
- GDP: $77B (2024)
- Key mandates: GRA e-levy, SSNIT statutory
- Mobile money: MTN MoMo + Vodafone Cash + AirtelTigo

**Ethiopia**
- GDP: $163B (2024, second largest Africa economy)
- Telebirr: state mobile money, 40M+ users
- Large manufacturing sector (textile, garment)

**Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia**
- Combined GDP: $100B+
- ZRA Smart Invoice (Zambia): mandatory e-invoicing
- Rwanda Revenue Authority: e-billing mandate

### Key features required
- Murabaha (cost-plus financing) — replaces conventional loans
- Ijarah (leasing) — replaces interest-bearing equipment finance

- Profit rate calculation (replaces interest rate calculation)
- Sharia compliance audit trail

**PgAppForge opportunity**: The `pgappforge/plugins/` directory already has an Islamic banking module foundation. This is an unclaimed market — no open-source platform has production-grade Islamic finance modules.

| Zambia | ZRA Smart Invoice | 2023 | Active — enforced |
| Ghana | GRA e-levy + e-invoicing | 2025 | Planning |
| Ethiopia | ERCA e-invoicing | 2025 | Planning |
